# Real Estate Calculator - Monetization Strategy & Results

## Monetization Channels

### 1. Mortgage Affiliate Partnerships

- **Average Commission**: $300-1,200 per funded loan
- **Conversion Rate**: 0.5-2% of calculator users
- **Strategy**: Partner with mortgage brokers offering competitive rates for investment properties
- **Implementation**: Strategic placement of mortgage pre-approval links after users calculate mortgage payments

### 2. Property Management Referrals

- **Average Commission**: $150-300 per signed client
- **Conversion Rate**: 0.3-1% of rental calculator users
- **Strategy**: Partner with nationwide property management companies with referral programs
- **Implementation**: Contextual recommendations based on property details entered

### 3. Real Estate Agent Referrals

- **Average Commission**: $500-2,000 per closed deal
- **Conversion Rate**: 0.1-0.5% of ROI calculator users
- **Strategy**: Partner with investor-friendly real estate agents specializing in investment properties
- **Implementation**: Property search tools integrated with affiliate links

### 4. Contractor Lead Generation

- **Average Value**: $20-50 per qualified lead
- **Conversion Rate**: 1-3% of rehab calculator users
- **Strategy**: Lead generation for contractors specializing in investment property renovations
- **Implementation**: "Find contractors" button after completing rehab estimates

### 5. Email List Monetization

- **Value**: $1-5 per subscriber per month
- **Conversion Rate**: 10-20% of calculator users
- **Strategy**: Build list of real estate investors for ongoing monetization
- **Implementation**: Offer PDF reports and additional calculators via email
